Brigade (brigade.lol): an AI team for your whole company

Brigade is an open-source (MIT) app that gives a company a team of AI teammates for support, marketing, sales and code. Each teammate is the official Claude Code or Codex, signed in with your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, so there is no second AI bill.

Visit brigade.lol Source on GitHub Live demo, no sign-up

What a teammate has

  • One job: support, marketing, sales, engineering, or one you write.
  • Its own computer, and the apps you give it (Gmail, GitHub, Stripe, Notion, Linear and others, or any MCP server), read-only or read-write.
  • A shared memory and library of how your team works.
  • Triggers and schedules, so work starts when an email, a webhook or a new issue arrives.

What still comes to you

For each teammate you choose which app actions wait for your yes (sending an email, a change in Stripe, a merge). Those requests land in one "Needs you" list, on the web and on your phone.

Pricing

Free on your own computer (macOS or Linux). Brigade Cloud is $99 a month per workspace and gives teammates a computer that keeps working when yours is off.
